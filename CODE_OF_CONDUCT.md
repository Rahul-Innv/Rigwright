# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, precise, and safe project environment. Participation must be free from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberate misuse of private or security-sensitive material.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable technical feedback without attacking the person.
- Respect privacy, provenance, licenses, and approval boundaries.
- Disclose uncertainty and limitations instead of fabricating proof.
- Keep examples generic and remove credentials, personal data, and machine-local paths.
- Accept maintainer decisions that protect users, evidence integrity, or lifecycle authority.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing private records, credentials, or identifying data.
- Circumventing review, permission, security, or promotion gates.
- Misrepresenting candidate behavior as installed, production-ready, or approved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens people, project integrity, or safety. Report conduct concerns through an existing trusted private channel with the repository owner. No public conduct-reporting contact is configured yet; do not expose sensitive details in a public issue.

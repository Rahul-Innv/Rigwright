# Third-party and captured material

The MIT license in this repository applies to original Rigwright material.

Frozen source captures, raw provenance records, and provider evaluation
evidence retain their original ownership and license terms; they are kept
privately by the project owner and are not part of this repository. The
Rigwright license does not relicense any captured or imported material.
Candidate distribution packages are built from an explicit allowlist and do
not include source captures, private provenance, provider records, or
imported package trees.
